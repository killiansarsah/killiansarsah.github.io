01 / MOBILE APP CASE STUDY MOBILE MONEY IN GHANA 100+ ACTIVE AGENTS

Mopay Agent

Fast, error-free mobile money transaction app built for field agents across Ghana

01 // Project Overview

In Ghana's fast-moving mobile money economy, field agents handle hundreds of daily Cash In, Cash Out, and Airtime transactions under extreme time pressure and bright outdoor conditions. The standard process relies on slow, timeout-prone carrier USSD menus (*170#, *110#) and error-prone physical record books.

Mopay Agent replaces this hassle with a fast, easy-to-use Android app. It automatically detects whether a phone number is MTN, Telecel, or AirtelTigo, calculates commission profits instantly, and runs USSD transactions automatically without manual dial errors.

01 / FAST TRANSACTIONS

One-Tap USSD Automation

Replaces slow multi-step dial codes (*170#, *110#) with one-tap actions, reducing transaction time from 45 seconds down to under 4 seconds.

02 / NETWORK DETECTION

Automatic Network Identification

Instantly identifies whether a customer's number is MTN, Telecel, or AirtelTigo as you type, preventing accidental transfers to the wrong network.

03 / OFFLINE RECORD KEEPING

Offline Records & Profit Tracker

Saves every cash-in, cash-out, and airtime sale right on the phone. Agents can search records and view daily profit summaries anytime, even without internet access.

Scam Number & Safety Alerts

Maintains a warning list of known scam numbers and suspicious transactions, alerting the agent before any money is sent.

02 / MOBILE APP CASE STUDY GOOGLE GEMINI AI VISUAL BUDGETING

FlowFi

Smart budgeting and personal expense tracker powered by Google Gemini AI

01 // Project Overview

Most budgeting apps are frustrating to use because typing in every receipt by hand takes too much time, and dense spreadsheets make it hard to see how much money you really have left.

FlowFi makes tracking expenses effortless and visual. Instead of rigid spreadsheets, spending categories are shown as animated water pools that lower as you spend. Powered by Google Gemini AI, you simply snap a photo of any receipt and FlowFi automatically reads the store, date, items, and total amount—with zero manual typing.
